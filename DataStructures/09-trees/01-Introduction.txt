Full Binary Tree: Every node has 2 children or 0 children
Complete Binary Tree: All levels are completely filled except the last level. 
					Last level filled from left to right.

Perfect Binary Tree: All leaf nodes are at the same level AND every internal node has 2 children.
					number of nodes with height h = pow(2, h) - 1
Balanced Binary Tree: for EVERY node, height(left) and height(right) differ by at most 1.
					(so the height stays about logN)
Degenerate Tree: (Skewed tree) all the nodes to the left / all the nodes to the right.
