# Important System Design Interview Questions

## Core Design Problems

1. Design a URL shortener such as Bitly.
2. Design a rate limiter.
3. Design a distributed cache.
4. Design a key-value store.
5. Design a unique ID generator.
6. Design a notification system.
7. Design a web crawler.
8. Design a search autocomplete service.
9. Design a file storage and synchronization service such as Dropbox.
10. Design an API gateway.

## Social and Communication Systems

1. Design a social media news feed.
2. Design a chat application such as WhatsApp.
3. Design a microblogging platform such as X (Twitter).
4. Design a photo-sharing platform such as Instagram.
5. Design a video-sharing platform such as YouTube.
6. Design a collaborative document editor such as Google Docs.
7. Design a video-conferencing system such as Zoom.

## Commerce and Financial Systems

## Fundamental Questions

1. How do vertical and horizontal scaling differ?
2. When should you use SQL instead of NoSQL, and vice versa?
3. What are database replication, partitioning, and sharding?
4. How do strong, eventual, and causal consistency differ?
5. What does the CAP theorem mean in practice?
6. What are ACID transactions, and when are they necessary?
7. How do optimistic and pessimistic locking differ?
8. How do you prevent race conditions and duplicate processing?
9. When should you use synchronous versus asynchronous communication?
10. How do queues, streams, and publish-subscribe systems differ?
11. What caching strategies and eviction policies would you use?
12. How do load balancers distribute traffic and detect failures?
13. How do you design APIs to be idempotent?
14. How do you handle retries, timeouts, and circuit breaking?
15. How do you eliminate single points of failure?
16. How do you plan for disaster recovery and data backups?
17. How do you monitor system health and define service-level objectives?
18. How do you secure data in transit and at rest?

## Questions to Address During Every Design

1. What are the functional and non-functional requirements?
2. What is the expected traffic, storage, bandwidth, and growth?
3. What API contracts and data models are required?
4. Which components belong in the high-level architecture?
5. Where could bottlenecks or single points of failure occur?
6. How will the system scale reads, writes, and storage?
7. What consistency, availability, and latency trade-offs are acceptable?
8. How will the system handle failures, retries, and recovery?
9. How will data be partitioned, replicated, cached, and indexed?
10. How will the system be secured, monitored, and deployed?
