# 1. Account plan and Region

## Check the account plan

On the AWS Console home page, open **Cost and Usage** and check:

- whether the account uses the Free or Paid plan;
- remaining credits;
- the credit expiry date.

For accounts created on or after July 15, 2025, the Free plan lasts until the earlier of:

- six months after account creation; or
- the available credits being exhausted.

Free Tier does not mean every resource is permanently free.

## Choose one Region

Use **Asia Pacific (Mumbai) — `ap-south-1`** throughout this course.

Select it from the Region menu in the top-right corner of the AWS Console.

```text
Dev EC2 + Prod EC2 + ECR + ALB = ap-south-1
```

Resources in another Region may not appear while Mumbai is selected. When something seems missing, check the Region first.
