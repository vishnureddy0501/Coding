# 3. Create a monthly budget

A budget sends warnings when spending approaches your chosen amount.

> A budget is not a hard limit. It does not automatically stop EC2 or ALB.

## Create it

1. Open **Billing and Cost Management**.
2. Choose **Budgets → Create budget**.
3. Select **Use a template**.
4. Select **Monthly cost budget**.
5. Name it `learning-monthly-budget`.
6. Enter the maximum amount you are comfortable spending.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Create the budget and verify the email if AWS asks.

The template alerts when actual spending reaches 85% and 100%, and when forecasted spending may reach 100%.

## Also check regularly

Open **Billing and Cost Management → Bills** to see charges by service and Region.

Budget data can be delayed, so do not depend on the email alone. Delete resources immediately after practice.
