# Module 6 practice

Add conversation only after Module 5 works.

Try:

```text
Show account-101.
What is its health?
List its five latest open tickets.
```

Then verify:

- each factual turn performs a fresh read;
- conversation ownership checks tenant and user;
- another user cannot reuse the conversation ID;
- changed permissions apply immediately;
- history is limited;
- a two-operation question never exceeds the call cap;
- truncated tickets are described as a subset.
